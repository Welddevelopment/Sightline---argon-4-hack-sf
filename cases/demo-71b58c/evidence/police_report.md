## PAGE 2: NARRATIVE

V1 was traveling east on US-27A in the outside (right) lane, coming down the grade from the crest west of NE 140th Ct. V2 had been traveling west on US-27A and was in the westbound left-turn lane at the NE 140th Ct median crossover. Witnesses described V2 as stopped or moving very slowly in the turn lane.

V2 began a left turn through the crossover and across both eastbound lanes toward NE 140th Ct. V1 did not brake, steer away or slow before impact. The front of V1 struck the right side of the semitrailer at about the trailer's mid-length, in the outside eastbound lane on the south side of the intersection. V1 passed beneath the trailer, and the trailer's lower edge sheared off V1's roof structure.

V1 continued east and left the roadway to the right (south) at a shallow angle. It crossed a drainage culvert, went through two wire fences and struck a wooden utility pole, breaking it. V1 rotated counterclockwise and came to rest about 910 ft east of the point of impact, perpendicular to the highway in the front yard of a residence near a driveway.

V2 continued through its turn and came to a stop on NE 140th Ct, south of the retail business on the corner.

No pre-impact tire marks were found on the roadway for either vehicle. The driver of V1 was pronounced deceased at the scene.
