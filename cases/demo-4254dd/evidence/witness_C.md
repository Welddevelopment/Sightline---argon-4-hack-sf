# WITNESS STATEMENT: Witness C

> **FICTIONAL WITNESS.** This statement was written for the Sightline demonstration. It is not a real person's statement. It is consistent with the physical facts in NTSB/HAR-17/02.

**Statement given to:** [State Highway Patrol, Trooper REDACTED]
**Date:** 05/09/2016, by phone, then written
**Witness location during the event:** stopped in my pickup in the westbound inside lane of US-27A, just west of the 140th Court crossover. I was waiting to make a U-turn through the next crossover. Marked position about 18 m west of the side road, in the westbound lanes.

---

I had pulled up and stopped because I was looking for the turnaround and wasn't sure I'd passed it. A big rig with a white refrigerated trailer was sitting in the westbound left-turn lane just ahead of me and to my left, at the crossover. I could hear the reefer unit running.

The truck started across. It was barely creeping when it pulled out, slower than a walk at first, then picking up a little as it swung across the eastbound lanes toward 140th. I was watching the truck, not the other side of the road, because I figured I'd go once it cleared.

I never heard brakes, tires or a horn before the bang. It was one loud crunch, and then I saw the car come out the far side heading east and leave the road toward the houses. The truck kept rolling through the turn and stopped down on 140th Court past the store.

It was a clear, sunny afternoon. The road was dry.

*Signed: Witness C (fictional)*
