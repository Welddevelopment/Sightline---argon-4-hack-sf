**Source:** National Transportation Safety Board, Highway Accident Report (car and tractor-semitrailer collision near Williston, Florida, May 7, 2016), **NTSB/HAR-17/02** (PB2017-102600), adopted September 12, 2017. Accident ID **HWY16FH018**.
- Report PDF: https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/HAR1702.pdf
- Investigation page: https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/Pages/HWY16FH018.aspx

These are the real physical facts only. No real names are used. "Vehicle A" is the car and "Vehicle T" is the tractor-semitrailer. ## Modeling assumptions used in case.json (not NTSB findings)
- The origin is the initial impact point: center of the eastbound outside lane, on the NE 140th Court centerline. The 109° skew is straightened to 90°.
- Event clock t=0 is the car at the crest. Impact is at t=10.43 s (345 m at 74 mph = 33.08 m/s, consistent with F28).
- Truck turn kinematics: 3.0 m/s rising to about 6.1 m/s at impact, with a 12 m turn radius. NTSB gives no truck speed (F19).
- Trailer 2.59 m wide, box 1.2–4.11 m above ground. These are standard reefer dimensions. Tractor 6.6 m long, 4.0 m tall.
- Car 4.97 × 1.96 × 1.445 m (typical full-size sedan).
- The retail building sits on the SE corner. Its footprint and height, the 6.1 m side-road width and the pole location are assumed. NTSB does not give them.
- The bench shelter in front of the store is fictional scene dressing.
