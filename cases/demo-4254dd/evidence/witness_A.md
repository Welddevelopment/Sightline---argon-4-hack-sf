# WITNESS STATEMENT: Witness A

> **FICTIONAL WITNESS.** This statement was written for the Sightline demonstration. It is modeled on the account of the real westbound eyewitness summarized in NTSB/HAR-17/02, pp. 1–2 and 29–30. It is not a real person's statement.

**Statement given to:** [State Highway Patrol, Trooper REDACTED]
**Date:** 05/07/2016, about 17:20 hrs, at the scene
**Witness location during the event:** driving westbound on US-27A in the outside (right) lane, about a quarter mile behind (east of) the tractor-trailer, at about 60 mph

---

I was heading west on 27A toward Bronson, doing about 60. There was a tractor-trailer with a white reefer trailer ahead of me, maybe a quarter mile up. It got over into the left-turn lane at 140th Court. I couldn't tell if he was completely stopped or just rolling real slow.

There was nobody else around. Looking down the road ahead of me, I saw a dark car come over the hill on the other side, eastbound, coming toward the intersection. That was before the truck started to turn.

Then the truck started across. Once the trailer was out across the eastbound lanes, it blocked my view of the car. I didn't see the car hit the trailer. I heard it, a loud bang, and then I saw the car come out from under the back side of the trailer, still going, with the top torn off. It went off the road to the south and down the shoulder. I heard it hit something farther down.

I never heard any brakes or a horn. The sun wasn't in my eyes. It was behind me and up high. Nothing was blocking my view up the road except the hill itself until the truck pulled across.

I pulled over on the westbound shoulder past the crossover and called 911.

*Signed: Witness A (fictional)*
